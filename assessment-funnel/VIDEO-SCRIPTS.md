# Assessment funnel videos

Two videos, both Andrew to camera. Shoot vertical or square for mobile (most cold
traffic watches on a phone with sound off at first, so caption everything).
Placeholder frames are already on the pages; swap instructions are in the HTML
comments (`index.html` hero, `results.html` video section).

---

## Video 1 — Landing page (45 to 60 seconds)

Job: stop the scroll, tell them the quiz is for them specifically, and make
starting it feel like a 3-minute no-risk win. Cold traffic has never heard of
you, so credibility has to land in one line, and the ask has to stay tiny.

**Structure:**

1. **Pattern interrupt / call out the avatar (0:00-0:08).** Open on the pain,
   name the person. "If you run a company and you're over 40, there's a number
   you've never seen, and it decides how the next 30 years go."
2. **The mechanism in one line (0:08-0:20).** Why their physical keeps saying
   fine while they feel off. "Your doctor grades you against 'normal.' Normal
   is the average of sick people. Half of heart attack patients had normal
   cholesterol on their last labs."
3. **Credibility, one line (0:20-0:30).** "I'm Andrew Martin. I'm a biologist,
   and for 10 years I've run deep diagnostics, over 1,000 biomarkers, for more
   than 500 founders and executives."
4. **What the assessment does (0:30-0:45).** "The assessment below takes 3
   minutes. 14 questions, the same intake I use with clients. You'll get a
   score from 0 to 100 and see which of your 5 systems is dragging: energy,
   metabolic, hormones, recovery, risk."
5. **CTA, low commitment (0:45-0:60).** "It's free, your answers stay private,
   and the worst case is you spend 3 minutes learning where you stand. Hit
   start."

**Delivery notes:** flat, confident, clinical-but-warm. No hype. You're a
scientist telling a peer something true, which for this audience beats an ad
read every time. One idea per sentence.

## Video 2 — Results page (60 to 90 seconds)

Job: they just saw their score and the flagged systems. This video makes the
score feel real, twists the knife on the cost of waiting, then makes the call
feel safe and worth 15 minutes. Speak to a score in the 50-75 range since
that's where most viewers will land.

**Structure:**

1. **Acknowledge the moment (0:00-0:10).** "So you've got your score. If it's
   lower than you expected, good. That reaction is the useful part."
2. **Interpret it (0:10-0:30).** "Here's what a score like that usually means
   when we run the actual labs. The symptoms you just reported, the crash, the
   waist, the drive, they're downstream. The numbers started moving 5 or 10
   years before you felt anything. By the time you feel it, it's been
   compounding."
3. **Twist the knife with a story (0:30-0:50).** Use Dustin without the name:
   "A 41-year-old construction founder took this same look under the hood. His
   doctor had just told him he was fine. His labs said diabetes. Triglycerides
   at 693. A year later he's down 47 lbs and the numbers look like a different
   man. The scary part is how normal he felt walking in."
4. **The gap, and the honest caveat (0:50-1:05).** "Your answers can point.
   Only blood can confirm. That's the gap between this quiz and the 1,000+
   markers we actually measure."
5. **De-risk the call (1:05-1:25).** "The next step is a 15-minute call. We
   review your score before we get on, tell you which labs would confirm or
   clear each flag, and give you a straight answer on whether we can help. No
   pitch at the end. If we're the wrong fit, I'll tell you who to go see
   instead."
6. **Close with the identity line (1:25-1:30).** "You track every number in
   your business. Go find out this one. Book the call."

**Delivery notes:** slower than video 1. This viewer already gave you 3 minutes
and their email; they want interpretation, so talk like it's minute 2 of a
consult. The "no pitch" promise is doing real work for show rates, so say it
plainly and mean it.

---

## Why these angles

- Both videos lead with the "normal vs optimal" contrarianism because the
  Mar-Jul 2026 sales call analysis showed that's the line that booked people
  from cold Instagram traffic.
- The Dustin story is the highest-contrast proof asset you have (fine → 693
  triglycerides) and it targets the exact fear cold traffic arrives with.
- The results video promises interpretation on the call, which matches what
  the call actually is (a diagnostic conversation) and protects show rates.
- Neither video mentions price. Pricing stays on the call, per funnel v2.
